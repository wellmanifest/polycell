# PC008 — Prohibited I/O in a pure cell

- **Severity**: ERROR
- **Producer**: `standard/conformance.py`
- **Reference**: [`docs/SPEC.md`](../docs/SPEC.md)

## Meaning

A cell declared with pure effects (`pure`) contains prohibited I/O operations or side-effecting
imports/calls.

## Inspection

Run `python3 standard/conformance.py --root .` to identify the cell and disallowed I/O pattern.

## Remediation

Remove I/O side effects from the `pure` cell or explicitly declare the required effects on
the cell contract and move side-effecting logic into an effectful cell.
