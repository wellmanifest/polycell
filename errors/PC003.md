# PC003 — Cell implementation gap, missing header, STALE, or STUB

- **Severity**: ERROR (`GAP`, missing header, `STALE`) / WARNING (`STUB`)
- **Producer**: `standard/conformance.py`
- **Reference**: [`docs/SPEC.md`](../docs/SPEC.md)

## Meaning

A declared cell implementation is missing (`GAP`), lacks its required managed header,
is out of date relative to its contract (`STALE`), or remains marked as a `STUB`
(reported as a conformance warning).

## Inspection

Run `python3 standard/conformance.py --root .` to list affected cell IDs and states.

## Remediation

Generate/update the managed cell scaffolding and complete the implementation matching
the current contract header and digest, then re-run conformance checks and contract tests.
